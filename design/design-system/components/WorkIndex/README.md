Work is a typographic list, not a grid of cards. Each project is one full-width row: number, title in the display face at 96px, then tags at the right.

- Rows: Filozo, Borderless Creatives (shown as Borderless), KEM Collections (KEM) and Fuze (four live apps). Tags come from the résumé only.
- Hover: every other row dims to 30% and the title slides 16px right. A rounded photograph of the project follows the pointer (260x330, `radius-lg`, tilted -3 degrees) and the ball becomes the label View. The preview fades and scales in and out; it never pops.
- Choose a row: the case study opens (see CaseStudy).
- No boxes, fills or borders; the rows are separated by space alone. Images are tech and AI photographs from the Photography group until real project screens replace them.
