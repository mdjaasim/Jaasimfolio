The site has no navigation bar, dock or sidebar. The header is the logo at top left and, at top right, the Dubai time and one word, "Index".

- The logo is `<JAASIM>` at the top of the page and morphs to `<J>` as the visitor scrolls (see Logo).
- Index opens a full-screen `ink` overlay that slides up with 40px top corners (900ms, ease (.7,0,.2,1)). The chapters are a giant list; they rise in one after another (50ms apart) and the current chapter carries a yellow dot. Email and links sit at the right. Closing slides it back down.
- Choosing a chapter closes the overlay and then glides to that chapter. Hovering a row brightens it and nudges it 16px right.
- There is no scroll indicator, no side thread and no hint text such as "Scroll". The story moves because the visitor scrolls.
