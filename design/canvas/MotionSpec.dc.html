Self-audit: passes

  • No green, blue or purple anywhere. Palette is bone, ink and one yellow.
  • No gradients. The film grain is a texture; the underline draws with a solid colour.
  • One yellow accent per viewport. The logo dot is the only constant; on yellow it turns ink.
  • No cards, sidebars or navbars. Navigation is one word and a full-screen overlay.
  • No invented metrics. Every number traces to the résumé.
  • Contrast: ink on bone 16.1:1, ink on yellow 13.2:1, muted on ink 7.4:1, muted on bone 5.2:1.

Self-audit: flagged

  • Yellow on bone is 1.2:1. Dots carry a thin ink outline; check the 16 px favicon and the cursor ball at 14 px.
  • Placeholders to replace: project year, live link, app screens, portrait, brand names in the SDK sheet, the availability wording and the fixed Dubai time 14:32.
