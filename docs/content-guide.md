# Content guide

- **Edit copy**: `src/content/en.ts`. Keep the voice: first person, plain, confident, sentence case, no emoji, no exclamation marks, no "passionate/innovative/cutting-edge". One Fraunces accent word per headline.
- **Case studies**: `src/content/work.ts`. `year` and `liveUrl` are optional; the row stays hidden until they are set.
- **Contact and links**: `src/config/site.ts`.
- **Swap a photograph**: replace `public/photos/<id>.jpg` (1600x1000, 16:10, dark and blue-toned) and update the alt and caption in `src/content/photos.ts`.
- **Licences**: the photos are Pexels stock (ids in `photos.ts`). Pexels licenses photos for free use, but confirm each photo's licence page before publishing and replace any you cannot use. Never caption stock as a client's work.
- **Placeholders**: app screens in the phone, the About portrait and `[YEAR]`/`[LIVE LINK]` are open. Do not invent metrics or testimonials.
